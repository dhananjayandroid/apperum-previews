Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before bringing your pup in.

Do you groom anxious or nervous dogs? +
Yes! We specialize in gentle 1-on-1 appointments with no noisy cages or rushing. We take extra time to build trust with nervous and rescue pets.
How long does a full grooming session take? +
Most full grooms take between 1 to 2 hours depending on breed, coat condition, and styling preferences. We contact you 15 minutes before completion for pickup.
What products do you use for baths? +
We use premium, plant-based hypoallergenic Australian pet shampoos and conditioners that nourish the skin without stripping natural protective oils.
How often should my dog be groomed? +
For fleece coats, doodles, and clipping breeds, we recommend every 4 to 8 weeks. For deshedding and hydrobaths, every 3 to 6 weeks keeps coats in prime condition.
